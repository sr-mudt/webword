    How to play

    Guess a five-letter term from web development in six tries. After each guess every letter gets a mark:

    • F correct: right letter, right place
    • E elsewhere: in the word, but in another place
    • X not in the word

    The marks use a symbol and a pattern, not only colour. After the game you learn what the term means.

    Webword is a teaching example for SE-501 Frontend Web Development (MUDT), inspired by the many word-guessing games. Word list: an-array-of-english-words (MIT licence).
